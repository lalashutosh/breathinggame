    Every tree has a story

    You plant a sprout. The garden does the rest: trees grow a stage each time a few more neighbours play.

    1. Play a game at your own pace. Breathe with the microphone, or hold a key.
    2. Earn one sapling for every game you finish.
    3. Plant it anywhere in the garden and watch it grow with everyone else's.

    About. MindPlay is a small collection of calm, body-aware games. Nothing here is a competition; the only leaderboard is the forest.

    Breath Dive can listen to your breathing through the microphone and read a Bluetooth heart-rate sensor (Polar, Wahoo, Garmin broadcast, Coospo and others). Both need a secure page (https or localhost); Bluetooth works in Chrome and Edge.
