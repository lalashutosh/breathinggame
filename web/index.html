    How it works About

    Every tree has a story

    You plant a sprout. The garden does the rest: trees grow a stage each time a few more neighbours play.

    1. Play a game at your own pace. Breathe with the microphone, or hold a key.
    2. Earn one sapling for every game you finish.
    3. Plant it anywhere in the garden and watch it grow with everyone else's.

    Grow a garden with a friend

    Everyone gets a garden of their own. Invite a friend and, for that run, every sapling they earn is planted in your garden; join theirs and yours go to them. You see who planted each tree and who is playing with you right now. Nobody keeps score: it is just a place to show up for each other.

    About. ΑΝΑΣΑ (anasa, Greek for “breath”) is one calm, body-aware game in three modes: minimalist, retro and immersive. Nothing here is a competition; the only leaderboard is the forest.

    Retro mode can listen to your breathing through the microphone and read a Bluetooth heart-rate sensor (Polar, Wahoo, Garmin broadcast, Coospo and others). Both need a secure page (https or localhost); Bluetooth works in Chrome and Edge.
